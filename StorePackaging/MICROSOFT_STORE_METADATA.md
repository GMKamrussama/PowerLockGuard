PowerLockGuard is an ultra-lightweight, offline smart power guard engineered specifically for developers, AI agent practitioners, and laptop users.

Are you tired of leaving your PC running hot all night while waiting for AI agents (Claude Code, Cursor, Aider, Codex, Copilot) or lengthy compile/training scripts to finish? PowerLockGuard solves this with intelligent task watchdog monitoring and instant charger disconnection protection.

════════════════════════════════════════
KEY FEATURES
════════════════════════════════════════

1. 🤖 SMART AI AGENT & WORK WATCHDOG
• Automatically detects active developer tools: Claude Code, Cursor, Aider, VS Code, Windsurf, Node.js, Python, Git, Cargo/Rust, dotnet, and build scripts.
• Smart Inactivity Buffer (1 to 10 min grace period): Prevents premature sleep while waiting for LLM API network responses.
• Automatically puts your laptop into Standby S3 Deep Sleep or Powers Off as soon as all monitored work concludes.

2. 🛡️ INSTANT CHARGER UNPLUG PROTECTION
• Unplugging your laptop's power adapter immediately triggers instant Deep Sleep (S3 Standby), Shutdown, or Lock.
• Zero battery drain, zero overheating in your backpack.
• Keeps all your open IDE tabs, terminal sessions, and uncommitted code safely preserved in RAM.
• 1-click pause switch when working on battery.

3. ⏰ 30-SECOND SAFETY WARNING COUNTDOWN
• Visual countdown screen with audible notification chime before triggering sleep or shutdown.
• Press 'Cancel' or hit 'Esc' anytime if you are still working at your desk.

4. 🪶 ULTRA-LIGHTWEIGHT & 100% PRIVATE
• Standalone native binary (~62 KB) with virtually zero background CPU (<0.1%).
• 100% offline. Zero telemetry, zero analytics, zero data collection.
• Native Windows System Tray integration with quick status controls.
